In this talk, I focused on the significance of applying Artificial Intelligence in the Humanities and the positive impact of interdisciplinary work in creating real social change.

I provided an introduction to processing Sign Language data, shared lessons learned from developing the First Bilingual Dictionary of Peruvian Sign Language-Spanish, and introduced several Artificial Intelligence models applicable to various scenarios in Sign Language. These included sign recognition, sign segmentation, sign-to-image generation, and sign language keypoint interpolation.
